FAQ

Quick answers about how the directory is maintained.

Is this a guarantee that a platform pays?

No. Platform policies, reward rates, offer availability and payout conditions can change. We list information to help you research; you remain responsible for evaluating each service.

Are the links referral links?

Some are. When a link is a referral link, the directory aims to disclose that relationship. A referral relationship does not mean a platform is guaranteed or endorsed.

Can I submit a platform?

Yes. Use the submission form above. We review submissions before adding them to the public directory.

Why are some platforms marked higher risk?

Risk can come from factors such as gambling-related products, token volatility, upfront spending, unclear reward economics or other material uncertainty. The label is a research aid, not financial advice.

How often should I re-check a platform?

Before using any platform, re-check its current terms, payout minimum, supported countries and withdrawal rules. These details can change without notice.
