FAQ

Do referral links earn this site money?

Some do. Referral relationships are disclosed and should never be interpreted as a guarantee that a platform is profitable or risk-free.

How much can I actually earn?

Most platforms here are best treated as supplemental earning opportunities. Bandwidth apps can be passive, while games and task sites generally require active time. Faucets typically pay very small amounts.

Why are some platforms Tier 3?

Tier 3 includes faucets, auto-earners and higher-risk or lower-value models. Being listed does not mean a platform is recommended for everyone.

Is this financial advice?

No. This directory is informational only. Research each platform yourself, read its current terms, and understand the risks before participating.

Important: Third-party platforms control their own services, payouts, terms and geographic availability. Crypto rewards can be volatile and some services carry financial, privacy, account or gambling-related risks. Never deposit more than you can afford to lose. VPN use should only be considered where permitted by the platform's current rules and applicable law.
